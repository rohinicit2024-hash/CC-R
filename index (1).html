Protection on your account

  • A payment password is required for every payment.
  • Every payment needs a one-time code that expires after 2 minutes.
  • Three wrong codes cancel the payment.
  • Payments cannot exceed your available credit.
  • You can only see your own transactions.

Stay safe

  • Never share your payment password or one-time codes with anyone.
  • Use a payment password that is different from your login password.
  • Log out when you finish, especially on a shared computer.
  • Check your history regularly and look for payments you do not recognise.
